Bundled Non-Cargo Components
----------------------------

These ship inside PwrDisk but are not crates, so cargo-about cannot list
them. Edit licenses/bundled.txt when one changes.

- disktree, by Tobi Lütke, which PwrDisk is a fork of. MIT; its notice is
  PwrDisk's own, in LICENSE.
- Velopack's native binaries, added to release packages by `vpk pack`
  1.2.161: Update.exe, Setup.exe and the launcher stub (Windows), the MSI's
  velopack_wix custom-action DLL, and UpdateMac (macOS). MIT, Copyright (c)
  2021 Caelan Sayler, Copyright (c) 2024 Velopack Ltd. | https://github.com/velopack/velopack
  The Rust crates compiled into them, with their license texts, are under
  "Velopack's Native Binaries" below (licenses/velopack.txt, written by
  scripts/release/velopack-notices.sh).
